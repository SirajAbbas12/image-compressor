Compress Images Strictly Below 20KB or 50KB

Need to upload photos or signatures to online government portals like SSC, UPSC, or Railway recruitment forms? Our web-based tool quickly reduces image sizes below 20 KB and 50 KB while keeping the photo clear.

Photo 20KB ya 50KB mein kaise kare (Steps)

  1. Upar "Drop an image here" par tap karke apni photo ya signature chunein.
  2. Target < 20KB ya Target < 50KB dabayein.
  3. Output format JPG rakhein (zyadatar forms ke liye).
  4. Download button dabayein aur file form mein upload karein.

100% Private Browser Processing

Your privacy matters. Unlike other resizers, your photos are compressed directly in your browser using client-side JavaScript. No images are ever uploaded to any server.

Kinke liye useful hai

Is tool se aap SSC, UPSC, Railway, bank, state exam aur passport photo ke online forms ke liye photo aur signature ki file size kam kar sakte hain. JPG, PNG aur WEBP teeno support hain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Photo ko 20KB mein kaise compress kare?

Upar tool mein photo upload karein, Target < 20KB dabayein, output format JPG rakhein aur Download dabayein. Tool photo ko 20KB ke andar le aata hai.

Kya meri photo kisi server par upload hoti hai?

Nahi. Photo aapke phone ya computer ke browser mein hi compress hoti hai. Koi image hamare server par nahi jaati.

SSC, UPSC ya Railway form ke liye kaun sa format use karein?

Zyadatar portal JPG ya JPEG maangte hain. Isliye JPG chunein, aur hamesha form ki instructions mein likhi size aur format ki shart check karein.

Kya ye tool free hai?

Haan, bilkul free hai. Koi sign-up ya installation nahi chahiye.

Kya signature ko bhi 20KB ya 50KB mein kar sakte hain?

Haan, signature ki image ke liye bhi wahi steps hain. Image upload karein, 20KB ya 50KB target chunein aur download karein.

PNG ko 20KB mein kyun nahi karta?

PNG lossless format hai aur browser mein uski quality kam nahi hoti. Isliye strict KB target ke liye tool PNG ko automatic JPG bana deta hai.
